Strategy for a long exam

  1. First pass: answer anything you know in under a minute. Flag the rest and move on.
  2. Watch the pace bar. If you fall two or more questions behind, skip harder questions sooner.
  3. Second pass: work the flagged questions, cheapest first.
  4. With 5 minutes left, bubble a guess for every blank. There is no penalty for a wrong guess on most MCQ exams, but a blank always scores zero.
