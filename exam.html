What you’ll get

  • Results by topic, your slowest questions, and an explanation for every answer.

Strategy for a long exam

  1. First pass: answer anything you know in under a minute. Flag the rest and move on.
  2. For a shared setup, do the work once (the full subset construction, or the whole partition refinement) and answer every question in the group from it.
  3. Read option (e) before choosing. The prof uses it for “None of the above” and “Both (a) and (b)”, and it is sometimes the answer.
  4. Watch the pace bar. If you fall two or more questions behind, skip harder questions sooner.
  5. Second pass: work the flagged questions, cheapest first.
  6. With 5 minutes left, bubble a guess for every blank. There is no penalty for a wrong guess on most MCQ exams, but a blank always scores zero.
