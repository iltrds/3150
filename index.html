Exam day

  • Bring HB pencils and an eraser.
  • Come early.
  • Write your FIRST AND LAST NAMES IN BOLD CAPITALS on both the bubble sheet and the question paper.
  • First pass: answer what you know, flag the rest, keep moving.
  • For a shared setup (an NFA or DFA table), work it out once and answer the whole group from it.
  • Read option (e). “None of the above” and “Both (a) and (b)” are sometimes right.
  • Before time runs out, bubble a guess for every question. A blank is always wrong.
